## 📌 About The Project

**DS Online Digital Studio** is a modern and responsive digital service platform designed to provide online assistance for government services, academic services, documentation, printing, digital payments, ticket booking, and other online work.

The platform is designed for:

* 👨‍🎓 Students
* 👨‍💼 Job Seekers
* 👨‍👩‍👧 Families
* 🏪 Local Customers
* 💻 Digital Service Customers

The website provides a simple interface where customers can explore services, check prices, view required documents, check updates, contact the studio, and get online assistance.
