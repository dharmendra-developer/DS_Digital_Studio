FAQ

Aadhaar download ke liye kya chahiye?

Aadhaar Number aur registered mobile OTP chahiye.

Scholarship apply karne me kitna time lagta hai?

Usually 10-15 minutes, documents ready hone par work fast complete ho jata hai.

PAN card apply ke liye kya documents chahiye?

Aadhaar card, mobile number, email ID, photo/signature details aur basic personal information.

Photo/signature resize work usually ₹30 se start hota hai. Exact charge size, format, aur form requirement ke hisaab se confirm kiya jata hai.

Haan, Tatkal railway ticket booking support available hai. Booking se pehle passenger details, ID proof, travel date, aur payment ready rakhein.

Cash aur UPI payment accepted hai. Online payment karne se pehle WhatsApp par exact amount confirm kar lena best rahega.
